Album Info

Release: January 17, 2020 (Posthumous Release)

Label: REMember Music / Warner Bros. Records

Producer: Jon Brion, who worked closely with Mac to complete the tracks based on their prior sessions and discussions.

Circles was specifically created as a companion piece to Swimming. Together, the two releases were designed to form a complete concept: Swimming in Circles. While Swimming focused on hip-hop structures and self-reflection, Circles pivoted almost entirely into a soft, introspective singer-songwriter/pop-soul style.

Famed composer and producer Jon Brion (who scored Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind) spent months carefully finishing the album after Mac’s passing. To honor Mac’s vision, Brion preserved Mac's original vocal takes and instrument tracks, often spending days fine-tuning subtle details Mac had requested in earlier conversations.

Despite being a posthumous project, Circles debuted at #3 on the Billboard 200 and moved 164,000 units in its first week—making it the highest first-week sales debut of Mac’s entire career.
